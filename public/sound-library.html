Current simulator · finite synthesized textures

Fountain and waterfall use fresh finite noise from the current src/audio/sizzle.js synthesizeSizzle API, not the old repeating texture. These fixed-seed dry renders use the same implementation; the live simulator varies the seed per emitter and spatializes the result. They are examples, not recordings of a complete live show.
