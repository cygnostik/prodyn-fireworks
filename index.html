    A small field guide.

    Tap a pictogram to fire it. Tap the sky to choose where your next shell opens. Each + adds that effect and colour to the Finale. Set a layer’s density to fill more of the sky.

    1—8 fire · Shift + 1—8 add a layer · Space fire selected · P pause · F finale · M sound. Touch controls do the same jobs.

    Sound starts only when you enable it. Distant flashes arrive before their reports. Hide the app and the show pauses; sound stays off when you return.

    Gentle mode softens bursts and starts still. You can pause or clear the sky at any time.
